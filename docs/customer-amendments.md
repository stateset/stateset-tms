# Customer amendments before pickup

An accepted customer agreement can need a new destination, date, equipment requirement, or inclusive base price. Freight records the replacement as a **separate linked quote**, with its own carrier offers, published terms, customer acceptance, and activation. Drafting, issuing, or accepting the replacement does not rewrite the current shipment or release its carrier. Activation changes the existing planned shipment and marks its preceding customer quote historical in one tenant transaction.

## Propose and activate

1. Open the booked quote and choose **Propose customer amendment**. Record a new quote reference and the actual reason. The customer identity stays fixed. Revise the full pickup/delivery plan, inclusive customer price, terms, and acceptance/activation deadline. Stop forms support two street address lines, contacts, phones, and instructions; unchanged details remain. Proposals are available before pickup, including while an original carrier is tendered/accepted/dispatched.
2. Record actual carrier offers against the proposed plan, explicitly select a qualified offer, and **Issue customer quote**. Current equipment and insurance must cover the amended delivery. Issuance freezes the actual issuer. The customer download identifies the agreement being amended and states that the old agreement remains effective until activation. Private carrier pricing, contact evidence, and commerce source snapshots are excluded.
3. Record the customer's actual acceptance, contact, external confirmation reference, and findings. For a Sync import, the acceptance form shows the exact verified source order being authorized. Cancelled, returned, or unknown source states require a deliberate **Customer explicitly authorizes this amended transport despite the commerce order status** confirmation. The API refreshes canonical source data before acceptance; a changed digest rejects stale confirmation instead of accepting unseen source changes.
4. Coordinate the original carrier through the established channel. Use proven-unsent **Withdraw unsent tender**, or obtain recorded external carrier release for a handed-off/accepted/dispatched tender. A new customer acceptance does not cancel or replace an existing carrier. The shipment must be planned and unassigned before activation, with no pending carrier release or unresolved custody incident.
5. Reconcile proposed/approved additional charges, carrier invoices/settlements, pending carrier invoice classifications, and non-void customer billing. The amendment cannot silently subsume those independent agreements. Reject or explicitly void/correct them through the appropriate existing workflow. If a changed stop has an active facility appointment, obtain and record its cancellation first. Unchanged stop appointments remain.
6. Choose **Activate customer amendment** before the new quote and selected carrier offer expire. Activation rechecks the original agreement version/procurement, the baseline plan/rates, the current shipment version, carrier qualification, and financial/appointment/custody prerequisites. It updates the same load ID/reference and plan/base rates, retains the old quote and tender snapshots, links the predecessor/successor, and makes exactly one agreement current. It creates no invoice, journal, payment, or new carrier assignment.
7. Tender separately under the new current agreement. The new 204 has its own carrier shipment identity and the amended addresses/requirements, while preserving the original load/BOL and commerce order identity. The old carrier's late evidence still follows [historical tender reconciliation](tenders.md); an amendment never re-labels an old response as the new carrier's acceptance.

The original agreement remains commercially effective while amendments are only proposed/issued/accepted; independent source and custody holds can still restrict new transport commitments. An externally recorded customer amendment remains evidence attested by the named staff member. Alternatively, publish the issued revision to the [customer portal](partner-portal.md#publish-customer-quotes-and-record-decisions) for an authenticated customer decision against its immutable document and current source status. Publication does not send email or activate the amendment; Freight does not independently authenticate signatures in external correspondence.

## Original history, stale proposals, and competing changes

The new quote's `amendment_of` points to its original quote/reference/version and procured offer. Each revision retains that basis and the full baseline shipment snapshot. Activation retains the immediate pre-activation shipment in `booking_source`; original import snapshots, documents, actual location, appointments/events, carrier tenders, invoices, receipts, and audit evidence stay recorded.

The preceding quote gains `superseded_by` with the successor, actor, and time. Its original revisions, offers, decisions, procurement awards, booking identity, and documents remain. Historical quotes cannot be reconfirmed, reprocured, revised, or used to revive the old agreement. The shipment API's `quote_binding` points only to the active successor. Customer documents for both original and replacement remain downloadable.

Multiple proposals can be negotiated against the same current agreement. Only one can activate. The database serializes activation; a competing request finds the original superseded and rolls back without changing the shipment or consuming its failed idempotency key. A further amendment must start from the current replacement, creating a linear effective-contract chain. Stale competing proposals remain visible as proposals and cannot branch the effective contract.

Changes to the original quote/procurement version invalidate a proposal's basis, including a later recorded carrier reconfirmation. To revise a still-current original basis, first record actual customer withdrawal for an accepted amendment, then **Revise quote** using the current parent and load versions. Record fresh offers, issuance, and actual customer acceptance for the new revision. Prior acceptance and source consent stay in the earlier revision. An already superseded parent cannot be silently rebased onto its successor: start a new amendment from that successor.

## Sync source consent

[Source controls](source.md) remain authoritative. Acceptance and activation of imported amendments independently fetch the canonical order through the configured tenant/API-key contract, outside database locks. The accepted revision stores the canonical raw snapshot, its business digest, and any explicit authorization despite status. At activation, the source grant is fenced by the observation version and freshness window; the current digest must match the customer's accepted snapshot. A later business change requires withdrawal/revision and renewed customer acceptance. Harmless tracking/attempt telemetry follows the existing source normalization rules; the full accepted raw snapshot remains retained and inspectable in quote history.

Activation appends a resolved source review bound to the **new freight plan**, linked to the accepted amendment and its actual customer evidence. Any earlier pending review is retained as superseded. The review observation identifies the canonical fetch actor/time; the customer decision keeps its acceptance actor/time. This permits the agreed amended plan to proceed without borrowing consent to a different, older plan. A stale or missing consent cannot clear a source hold.

Freight does not rewrite commerce items, totals, source status, original snapshots, or tracking merely because an amendment activates. Tracking facts from a preceding withdrawal or other pre-pickup step remain `local_only`; they cannot mark an unshipped commerce order shipped or block later actual pickup/delivery tracking. Known unsafe tracking statuses and freight cancellation payloads remain held, even after explicit customer transport authorization. The Sync tracking endpoint's unconditional `SHIPPED` behavior and the absence of an atomic reservation/conditional mutation still apply. A fresh GET cannot eliminate every change between verification and a remote handoff.

## API

All writes require `operations.write`, `Idempotency-Key`, and authenticated audit subjects. Readers can inspect both agreements and their source/history; finance-only and integration-only roles cannot attest customer acceptance or activate a contract.

- `POST /v1/quotes/{original_id}/amendments`: `{expected_version, expected_load_version, reference, plan, sell_cents, valid_until, terms, reason}`. `expected_version` is the current original quote version. Returns a new draft quote. The plan uses normal quote fields/full stops; customer ID must remain unchanged.
- Existing `/offers`, `/offers/withdraw`, `/issue`, `/decision`, `/revise`, `/book`, and `/statement` routes operate on the new quote. Its pending status follows the normal quote lifecycle.
- Imported amendment acceptance adds `source_digest` and `authorize_transport` to `/decision` with `decision:"accept"`. The digest must be the actual displayed current check; the API obtains canonical data itself, not a client snapshot. Explicit authorization must be `true` for cancelled/returned/unknown source status. Non-imported amendments omit these fields.
- Amendment `/revise` additionally requires `expected_parent_version` and `expected_load_version`, rebasing a new revision against the same still-active parent. Accepted proposals require actual customer withdrawal first.
- Amendment `/book` requires `{expected_version, expected_load_version}` and activates on the existing shipment. It never creates a second load. Successful lost-response replay returns the original activation; changing the request under an already committed key returns 409.
- Workspace quote revisions add optional `amendment_of` and `source_consent`; quotes add optional `superseded_by`. Source decisions can carry `quote_id` for the accepted amendment. Old records deserialize with absent fields defaulted, but activation never treats missing consent as authorization.

Migration `007_quote_amendments.sql` advances storage format **4 → 5**, preserving earlier data and file bytes. The database fences previous writers so they cannot discard amendment/source links or revive superseded agreements. Follow the [storage deployment/rollback procedure](storage.md).

## Boundaries and qualification

These amendments replace a base agreement before pickup, after safe carrier coordination. They do not perform in-custody rerouting, release goods from a carrier, change already posted invoices, or transfer the customer entity. Existing [additional charges](charges.md), [customer supplements](customer-supplements.md), [carrier adjustments](payables.md), and [cancellation fees](cancellations.md) handle their respective later agreements. The supported `Tender204` fields carry pickup date, weight, parties/addresses, BOL and order references. Carrier correspondence must cover the full agreed delivery timing, equipment/commodity requirements, prices, and any contact/instruction details outside that payload. The inspected EDI gateway supports original 204s; automated partner-specific change/cancellation messages require a separately qualified contract. There is no automatic merchant amendment, bank action, or customer delivery of documents.
