# Customer quotes and carrier procurement

Reusable [carrier lane agreements](carrier-contracts.md) can source the exact inclusive price for a draft offer or safe replacement award. Applicability is evaluated against the quote's actual plan, and each use still requires fresh carrier capacity confirmation. The offer retains the exact source agreement revision/digest; later catalogue changes do not rewrite the separately confirmed offer.

Freight retains a customer's proposed inclusive rate in the quote plan's currency, actual carrier offers, and their lane/terms together before booking. A recorded offer, issued customer quote, or customer acceptance does not assign carrier custody or post an invoice. Booking creates one planned shipment or binds the accepted rates to an explicitly selected existing planned shipment, retaining its identity and source order. Carrier acceptance continues through the existing [tender workflow](tenders.md).

## Quote and book a shipment

1. Open **Quotes → Create quote**. Select a new shipment or an existing unassigned planned shipment. For an existing shipment, the original plan is read-only; revise it explicitly in the load workspace before quoting when needed. Set the customer, full pickup/delivery addresses and dates, commodity, equipment, weight, distance, inclusive customer price, UTC acceptance/booking deadline, and commercial terms. FTL/LTL and the platform's existing equipment types are supported. Creating a quote does not create a shipment, integration handoff, or journal.
2. **Record carrier offer** from actual carrier correspondence. Record the carrier, exact amount, UTC validity, actual contact, source reference, and findings. Carrier equipment and current insurance must cover the quoted delivery date; carrier SCAC/partner identities must remain consistent with the recorded snapshot. Each offer is independently identified. Recording another offer does not overwrite the first or select the cheapest automatically. References identify one carrier offer within that revision; use a distinct source/revision reference when an actual carrier revision changes the offered amount.
3. **Issue customer quote** and explicitly select an actual carrier offer. The customer deadline cannot exceed that carrier offer's validity. Configure the actual billing issuer in Finance first; issuance freezes that issuer's identity. Download the customer document to inspect the lane, price, validity, and terms before using your actual delivery channel. It excludes carrier costs, carrier identity, and private procurement evidence. Issuance does not send the document externally.
4. Record actual customer acceptance/decline with the contact, external confirmation reference, and findings. Customer acceptance requires the live issued revision and a valid, unwithdrawn carrier offer. An expired quote cannot be accepted or booked. The quote terms/document explicitly use the deadline for **both acceptance and booking**; this workflow does not treat acceptance as an unlimited reservation of carrier capacity.
5. **Book quoted shipment** before that deadline. Booking rechecks offer validity and current carrier qualification, then creates one planned load or updates only the agreed rates on the selected existing planned load. The quote, lane, customer, current source version, and eligibility must still match. Booking an existing load retains its load ID/reference, source order UUID/number/snapshot, documents, and tracking connection; the pre-booking load/rates are also retained in `booking_source`. It does not import or create a duplicate shipment. Competing accepted quotes cannot both claim the same source load. Both paths use the exact selected buy/accepted sell rates. It retains the original quote, decision, and procurement award. Lost responses replay the original booking; competing requests cannot create a second shipment.
6. **Open booked shipment → Tender**. The carrier selector is restricted to the current procured carrier. The API checks that carrier, rates, offer validity, and live qualification again. Tendering creates the existing independent carrier shipment identity and 204 handoff. A price quote does not act as a 990 acceptance, pickup, delivery, POD, invoice, or payment.

The customer price may be below a carrier offer; this module records the actual commercial decision and does not impose a tenant margin-approval policy. Rates are inclusive integer minor units in the plan's [currency](multi-currency.md) (USD by default), limited to the same positive supported amount range as the freight workflow. No tax or automatic fuel/accessorial pricing is inferred from the lane.

## Native customer publication and decisions

After issuance, **Publish to customer portal** exposes that exact customer revision and frozen document to its configured counterparty account. The customer signs into `/portal`, inspects the plan, inclusive sell price, terms, and deadline, and records actual acceptance, decline, or withdrawal. Decisions retain the authenticated customer identity and exact document hash. The original document stays unchanged after acceptance, booking, and later revisions. Drafts, procurement costs, carrier contacts, and staff notes remain private.

Native acceptance does not book or activate a shipment. Operations uses the existing booking/activation controls and rechecks capacity, source state, and custody. An imported amendment requires fresh canonical source verification and explicit customer transport authorization for cancelled/returned/unknown states. Source verification exposes a scoped order identity/status summary, not raw commerce data. See [customer portal publication and acceptance](partner-portal.md#publish-customer-quotes-and-record-decisions) for exact endpoints, access, history, and recovery.

## Revisions and withdrawals

Each revision retains its own lane/customer snapshot, issuer snapshot, price, terms, validity, actual carrier offers, issuance, and customer-decision history. A new draft revision starts with no selected carrier offer or customer acceptance. Prior customer documents remain downloadable by revision number; private offers and customer decisions remain visible in the prior-revision history.

Draft, issued, declined, and withdrawn quotes can be revised with an explicit reason. An accepted unbooked quote must first record actual customer withdrawal/coordination before revision. Withdrawal appends evidence; it does not erase the previous acceptance. A carrier offer can be withdrawn with actual carrier evidence while the quote is unbooked. An issued/accepted quote with a withdrawn selected offer cannot be booked; coordinate the customer agreement and issue the correct revision.

Booked quotes retain their customer plan and base sell agreement. Generic **Edit plan** is unavailable for their shipments; a silent generic plan/rate rewrite is rejected by the API. Different customer plans/prices require explicit commercial amendment rather than repurposing an old acceptance. Use the implemented [customer amendment workflow](customer-amendments.md): a separate linked proposal/acceptance activates on the same safely released planned shipment and retains the original agreement; ordinary documented additions, delivered-load supplements, carrier release, and cancellation fees use their established workflows.

## Reconfirmation and replacement procurement

For a booked planned shipment, **Reconfirm carrier rate** records actual evidence extending the current carrier's validity at the same plan and price. Reconfirmations are tied to that exact procurement offer, so an earlier carrier's extension cannot qualify a replacement. Original validity/price evidence remains unchanged. Expired offers hold tendering until an actual extension or new procurement is recorded.

**Record replacement procurement** records a different confirmed carrier cost while retaining the customer's accepted lane and sell amount. The shipment must be unchanged, planned, and unassigned. An existing tender must first follow proven-unsent withdrawal, carrier decline, or externally confirmed release. Pending/active carrier custody, unresolved incidents, approved additional charges, carrier invoices, or non-void customer billing block this replacement. The operation retains the preceding award/offer, records the new carrier snapshot/contact/reference/rate/reason, updates the planned load's buy amount and version, and appends audit evidence. It posts no journal and does not tender automatically.

The original customer revision and initial carrier price remain, alongside each subsequent procurement award. Prior tender snapshots keep their original carrier/cost/identity. The replacement tender uses the new carrier/cost and independent wire reference; late former-carrier 990/214/210 evidence continues through the existing historical-carrier reconciliation rules. Reconfirmation is available before tendering; a booked carrier commitment cannot be withdrawn by merely retracting its old quote offer.

## Access, persistence, and integrations

Staff quote mutations require `operations.write`, authenticated audit subjects, and `Idempotency-Key`. Existing-quote commands require expected quote versions. Reconfirmation/replacement also require the expected load version. Every staff reader can inspect/download quotes; viewer, finance-only, and integration roles cannot assert customer acceptance or procurement. Client-supplied role/actor fields do not grant authority. Clerk-mode requests and downloads retain their organization binding.

Quotes and booking/load creation commit in the existing tenant transaction. Quoting was introduced in PostgreSQL storage format **3** by migration `005_quotes.sql`; format **4** added retained source review; format **5** added customer amendments; format **6** added paid-customer credit/refund liabilities; format **7** added immutable carrier rate documents and external handoff evidence; format **8** added explicit partner publications and native carrier decisions; format **9** added native carrier movement/POD evidence; format **10** added immutable customer quote publications and authenticated customer decisions; format **11** added controlled original-file sharing and download authorizations; format **12** added immutable carrier release publications, native responses, and separate application evidence; format **13** added reusable carrier lane agreements and exact procurement source revisions; format **14** added ordered multi-stop plans and immutable actual service/correction evidence; format **15** enforces shared execution chronology and final-tracking holds; current format **16** retains native movement corrections and effective actual-time validation/writeback while preserving earlier business data and migration history and preventing older APIs from discarding records. Quote writes do not contact carrier/customer services while holding a tenant lock. External carrier/customer correspondence remains attested by the named operator; native customer decisions additionally bind the authenticated customer subject to an explicitly published document hash; this implementation does not independently authenticate signatures in external correspondence.

The inspected `stateset-edi` `Tender204` model exposes shipment/SCAC, payment method, dates, BOL, weight, parties, and orders; it has no quote-price field. Freight keeps price evidence locally and uses the supported original 204 contract. Use [carrier rate confirmations](carrier-rate-confirmations.md) for exact-tender price/stop/terms documents and retained external handoff evidence. Automated delivery and partner-specific electronic pricing/cancellation formats require their own qualified integration. The codec harness books a quote, separately tenders it, and compiles the generated request using the actual sibling library.

Use the existing sync-order import first, then select that planned shipment in the quote form. Its canonical source order is fetched through the configured Sync tenant/API-key contract at import and again before existing-source booking and replacement procurement. Quote creation/revision captures the local source plan/version; booking checks fresh canonical source observations/review authorization plus the live local source version, matching plan, and absence of other quoted/carrier/financial commitments. The source order snapshot and tracking ownership remain. Quote prices/private procurement evidence are not written into sync-server order totals or tracking. Booking does not reserve or lock the remote commerce order; later upstream amendments/cancellations produce retained [source reviews](source.md) and require explicit customer reconciliation before new transport commitments. [Carrier contract sourcing](carrier-contracts.md) now retains exact lane agreements and requires separately confirmed capacity; customer sell-side contracts, formula pricing, and remote order reservation/amendment feeds remain further work. No sibling repository, live carrier, commerce customer, or external bank is changed by local qualification.

## API

- `POST /v1/quotes`: `{reference, plan, sell_cents, valid_until, terms, planned_load_id?, expected_load_version?}`. An existing source requires its current load version and an exactly matching plan. `plan` contains `customer_id, mode, equipment, commodity, weight_lbs, miles, origin, destination`; stop fields follow the normal load plan. No caller-selected buy rate is accepted here.
- `POST /v1/quotes/{id}/revise`: `{expected_version, plan, sell_cents, valid_until, terms, reason, expected_load_version?}`. Existing-source binding remains fixed; its current plan/version must match.
- `POST /v1/quotes/{id}/offers`: `{expected_version, carrier_id, amount_cents, valid_until, contact, reference, note}`.
- `POST /v1/quotes/{id}/offers/withdraw`: `{expected_version, offer_id, contact, reference, note}`.
- `POST /v1/quotes/{id}/issue`: `{expected_version, offer_id}`.
- `POST /v1/quotes/{id}/decision`: `{expected_version, decision: "accept"|"decline"|"withdraw", contact, reference, note}`.
- `POST /v1/quotes/{id}/book`: `{expected_version, expected_load_version?}`. The load version is required for an existing source and omitted for standalone booking.
- `POST /v1/quotes/{id}/reconfirm`: `{expected_version, expected_load_version, valid_until, contact, reference, note}`.
- `POST /v1/quotes/{id}/reprocure`: `{expected_version, expected_load_version, carrier_id, amount_cents, valid_until, contact, reference, note, reason}`.
- `GET /v1/quotes/{id}/statement?revision=N`: protected printable HTML download; omit `revision` for the current revision.

Workspace responses include optional amendment/supersession/source-consent links (see [amendments](customer-amendments.md)), `quotes`, their effective expiry/status, complete revision/procurement/decision history, and each booked load's `quote_binding`. Timestamps use RFC3339; the browser's explicitly labeled deadline controls use UTC. New quotes require pickup today or later. Changing a published quote produces a new revision rather than rewriting prior evidence.

Local domain qualification checks exact selection of a 125,001-cent carrier offer over an unselected 110,001-cent offer, a 150,001-cent accepted customer price, and a safely procured 130,001-cent replacement while the original 125,001-cent offer/tender and customer sell price remain. Tests cover expiry, identity/qualification changes, withdrawal, accepted-revision history, customer-document escaping/privacy, missing issuer, immutable booked rates, competing PostgreSQL booking and lost-response replay, tenant isolation/reconnect, actual gateway codec compatibility using a quoted Sync import, competing quotes claiming one existing shipment, matching/stale-source guards, preserved import snapshots, original-order tracking without financial leakage, role denial, and operator/browser workflows. These are synthetic protocol/business scenarios; they do not certify a live carrier's agreement or transport.
