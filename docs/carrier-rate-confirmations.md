# Carrier rate confirmations

The supported `stateset-edi` 204 request contains shipment identity, stops, dates, BOL, weight, and order references, but no carrier price. Freight issues a separate carrier-facing rate document against the exact tender attempt. Its document, revisions, and external handoff evidence remain separate from EDI transport, carrier acceptance, physical custody, and payment.

## Operator workflow

1. Configure the actual issuing legal name, address, and contact under **Freight audit → Billing identity**. The carrier document uses those identity fields, excluding customer payment instructions. Synthetic demo identity stays explicitly labeled.
2. Open a tendered, accepted, or dispatched shipment before pickup and select **Issue carrier confirmation**. Record the actual carrier terms and an issuance reason. The amount derives from the current carrier buy agreement plus separately approved carrier additions; the caller cannot supply a replacement amount, carrier, issuer, HTML, digest, or actor.
3. Resolve proposed carrier charges, release/custody holds, and any commerce source review first. For a Sync import, the API fetches the canonical order outside the database transaction and fences that exact observation at commit. Unavailable or changed/cancelled source orders cannot silently produce new transport commitments. Existing source snapshot/order identity and commerce totals remain unchanged.
4. Download the issued HTML attachment. Inspect, print/save as PDF, or share through your actual operating channel. It contains the exact carrier name/SCAC/MC, independent tender shipment ID, load/order references, base and approved carrier amounts, full stop addresses, contacts, instructions, facility windows with timezone, equipment, commodity, weight, issuing identity, terms, revision, and issuance time. Customer sell price, margin, customer identity, private procurement/charge evidence, original commerce payloads, and bank instructions are excluded. Enter only carrier-facing information in terms and operating instructions.
5. Select **Record actual handoff** only after the external communication occurred. Record its actual UTC sent time, carrier contact, channel (`email`, `portal`, or `other`), original reference, and findings. Optionally select a stored evidence file from that shipment. This records an operator's inspection of external evidence; Freight does not send a message or independently authenticate it.
6. Review **Issued snapshot and history** for the original document hash, terms, reason, handoff files, actors, dates, and corrections. A handoff does not accept the load, post a charge, start EDI transmission, or initiate payment. Record actual carrier acceptance separately through the existing 990/manual acceptance workflow.

The issued UTF-8 HTML bytes and their SHA-256 digest are stored together. Downloads return those original bytes and verify the digest; later master-data, rates, appointments, templates, or issuer changes do not regenerate historical documents. The workspace response omits HTML and exposes metadata/snapshots plus `current` and `basis_changed`. A changed current snapshot prompts a revision; historical documents stay downloadable with their own revision and issuance date. Do not share tenant-wide workspace credentials with a carrier; this is a staff workflow, not an external partner portal.

## Revisions and commitment safety

**Issue revised confirmation** requires the current load version and the exact preceding document ID for that tender. Each new revision captures the current agreement/stop/issuer basis and explicit terms/reason, with a retained `supersedes_id`. Simultaneous attempts cannot both replace the same version. A replacement tender has its own document chain and wire identity. Existing assigned loads can adopt a clearly marked legacy tender snapshot; issuance does not invent a historical tender or delivery time.

Issuance alone leaves proven-unsent withdrawal available when the existing EDI evidence permits it. **Publish to carrier portal** separately makes the exact document available to the configured carrier account and blocks unsent withdrawal, including without a recorded view or EDI attempt. See [native partner acceptance and scope controls](partner-portal.md). A recorded non-retracted external handoff on **any revision for that tender** blocks local unsent withdrawal, even if the EDI outbox still has zero attempts. Request and record actual carrier release before cancellation/reassignment. Retrying or revising a document cannot erase the communication history.

An operator may discover an actual handoff after a document was superseded, its current basis changed, its tender retired, or release was requested. Freight retains that fact and raises **Coordination required**. It holds new tendering/dispatch/release confirmation and the existing custody-sensitive finance gates. Open the retained handoff, contact the carrier, and **Resolve handoff coordination** with the actual contact, external confirmation reference, and findings. Resolution retains current ownership; it does not itself release a carrier, accept an offer, settle custody, or create a liability. Record any resulting verified operational action separately.

Use **Correct erroneous handoff** only when the recorded communication was erroneous. Retraction appends its reason, actor, and time; the original entry and evidence remain. It does not recall a message. A corrected observation can be recorded afterward; duplicate active channel/reference pairs are rejected without regard to surrounding spaces or ASCII case. A genuine sent document must not be retracted to force unsent withdrawal.

## API, evidence, and access

All writes require `operations.write` and `Idempotency-Key`. All tenant staff readers can inspect/download their tenant's records; finance/integration/viewer roles cannot issue, attest, retract, or resolve a handoff. Tenant and actor are server-owned. Body shapes reject unknown fields.

- `POST /v1/loads/{id}/rate-confirmations`: `{expected_load_version, expected_previous_id?, terms, reason}`. Terms require 1–4000 characters; reason requires 1–200. Missing/null predecessor is valid only for the first revision of that tender.
- `GET /v1/rate-confirmations/{id}/statement`: exact original HTML attachment, private/no-store, restrictive CSP, nosniff, and `X-Content-SHA256`. Corrupt stored HTML fails instead of being regenerated.
- `POST /v1/rate-confirmations/{id}/handoffs`: `{expected_version, expected_load_version, sent_at, carrier_contact, channel, reference, note, document_id?}`. `sent_at` is RFC3339 UTC, at/after document issuance and no later than current server time. All text evidence fields require 1–200 characters.
- `POST /v1/rate-confirmations/{id}/handoffs/retract`: `{expected_version, expected_load_version, handoff_id, note}`.
- `POST /v1/rate-confirmations/{id}/handoffs/resolve`: `{expected_version, expected_load_version, handoff_id, carrier_contact, confirmation_reference, note}`. Only an unresolved, non-retracted coordination hold can be resolved.

Issuance and handoff changes advance the load version; handoff/retraction/resolution also advance the document's metadata version. The original document bytes/hash never change. Successful idempotency replay returns its original result without another source fetch or posting. Stale requests leave no candidate state.

Approved carrier-charge files are checked inside the issuance transaction. Handoff files bind to the same tenant/load, must not be rejected, retain their original digest, and have their exact bytes checked inside the handoff transaction. Missing/corrupt files roll back state, audit, versions, and idempotency receipt. Generic rejection cannot invalidate a file used by retained handoff history, including a retracted record.

Migration `009_rate_confirmations.sql` advances storage format 6 to **7** without rewriting business history or bytes. Older writers are fenced. See [storage deployment and rollback](storage.md).

## Qualification and boundaries

The independently expected 125,001-cent carrier agreement appears as USD 1,250.01 while the 150,001-cent customer price stays absent. A separately approved 1,234-cent addition produces a new 126,235-cent revision; the previous HTML remains byte-for-byte unchanged. Tests cover two address lines, escaped instructions/terms, timezone windows, issuer/master-data changes, pending-charge/source/release holds, stale/forged requests, legacy adoption, actual handoff blocking unsent withdrawal, old-document review holding dispatch, corrections, file ownership/locking, and replay.

PostgreSQL qualification corrupts real charge/handoff bytes, confirms complete rollback, restores them, and reuses failed keys. Competing API instances commit only one issuance and one handoff against the same versions; reconnect preserves exact HTML/hash, evidence, empty financial journals, and tenant isolation. Source HTTP qualification verifies canonical lookup before issuance, viewer denial before IO, replay without refetch, and retained cancellation review. The actual sibling codec harness checks the carrier document against the shipment identity and selected rate accompanying its real 204. Production browsers cover issuance/download/hash, evidence, retraction, revisions, coordination resolution, mobile controls, nested-dialog Escape behavior, and viewer denial.
